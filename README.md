I hold a **Master's degree in Wireless Intelligent Sensor Networking (WISENet)** from the **African Centre of Excellence in Internet of Things (ACEIoT), University of Rwanda**, formerly Kigali Institute of Science and Technology (KIST).

My postgraduate studies provided a foundation in wireless communication, computer networking, Internet of Things technologies, sensors, embedded systems, and networked applications. My professional interests and work in IT and network administration have strengthened my interest in reliable and secure enterprise infrastructure.

I am developing further specialisation in **cybersecurity**, particularly network defence, infrastructure security, security monitoring, vulnerability assessment, and the protection of connected systems.
